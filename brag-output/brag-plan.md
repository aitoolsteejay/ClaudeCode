# Brag Plan: Myntmore

## What is this app?
Myntmore is a Mumbai-based, AI-powered B2B outbound agency - it runs cold email, LinkedIn outreach, and ABM for clients, and its own site backs that up with free AI tools, a 30-term glossary, and a real content system, not just a services page.

## The angle
Most agency sites are a hero, three feature cards, and a "book a call" button. This one is a working system: real numbers that count up on load, a free AI tool that actually builds you an ICP on the spot, and a content library deep enough to be its own product. The video's angle is "watch the site work, not just look nice" - fast cuts through proof, product, and depth, landing on the same line the site itself makes: we fill the pipeline, you close the deals.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The headline slams in on the cream background: **"We Fill Your Pipeline."** - full black, full weight - with the gold hand-drawn underline whipping in underneath a beat later. No logo yet. The line has to read as a claim, not a tagline.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The three hero stat counters ticking up live: $120M+ pipeline created, 18 days to first booked meeting, 12K+ meetings booked - the credibility hit, delivered as numbers actually counting, not printed as static text.
- The ICP Builder tool actually running: a one-line business description typed into a real input, then an ICP card (role, company size, pain point) and a value-proposition line assembling in front of you.
- A fast flash-cut through the content system: glossary term cards, a case study card, a blog card, snapped in quick succession - the "there's way more here" beat.

## Outro / punchline
Wordmark settles in on cream with the gold underline echoed under it, then the line that closes the loop: **"You Close the Deals."** - followed by the real CTA copy from the site, "Book Your Free Strategy Call," held just long enough to read.

## User flow worth showing
The ICP Builder tool (`/tools/icp-builder`) - free AI tool that builds a B2B/D2C ICP and value proposition from a business description. Flow: type a one-line business description → hit generate → an ICP card and value-prop line materialize. This is the centerpiece "product actually doing its thing" scene; the ROI calculator and LinkedIn optimizer exist too but the ICP Builder is the most visually legible in one pass (input → generated structured card, not just a number).

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: lively, a little fast-paced - energetic agency showcase, quick punchy cuts, confident and proof-driven rather than silly or aggressive. This is a serious B2B brand (cream/near-black/gold, not startup-meme energy), so the speed comes from tight cuts and snappy holds, not from jokes or chaos.
- Interpretation: 5 scenes, comfortable-but-quick pacing (holds land right at the reading floor, never longer), hard-ish crossfades between scenes instead of soft dissolves to keep momentum, one clear beat of "watch the AI tool actually run" as the emotional peak.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 19s target

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#F8F6F2` (warm cream)
- Accent: `#F5B731` (gold - used for the hand-drawn underline, CTA borders, stat highlights)
- Text: `#0A0A0A` (near-black, primary) / `#6B6B6B` and `#8C8279` (secondary/muted)
- Border/hairline: `#E8E2D9` (warm tan)
- Display font: Inter (black/bold weights for headlines)
- Body font: Inter (regular/medium)
- Accent/handwritten font: Caveat (600/700) - used sparingly for hand-annotated call-outs on the real site
- Strongest visual element: the gold hand-drawn wavy underline beneath the hero headline, paired with the three stat counters counting up inside a bordered pill/card row - this is the site's most distinctive, least-generic-SaaS moment (no blue gradients, no stock illustration)

## Share copy (draft)
We turned Myntmore into more than a services page - a 30-term glossary, free AI tools that actually build your ICP, and the same system that's booked 12K+ meetings and $120M+ in pipeline. Built to convert, not just to look nice.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, energetic bed with light rhythmic drive - sparse professional accents on top, not a wall of SFX
- Music: upbeat, modern instrumental with a light percussive pulse; confident rather than aggressive (no trap/EDM chaos, no elevator-corporate blandness either)
- Music treatment: starts at near-full energy under the hook (this video has no slow build-in), holds through the middle, small dip/duck under the ICP Builder typing beat so the "typing" SFX reads clearly, swells slightly for the outro card, clean fade on the last frame
- Music cue guidance: no bundled track chosen yet - to be detected at composition time via `npx hyperframes beats`; target 3 strong-cue alignments: (1) the underline whip-in at the end of the hook, (2) the first stat counter landing on its final value, (3) the ICP card's first tag snapping in. Sequential reveals (stat cards, ICP tags, content flash-cuts) should snap to every-other-beat spacing, not every beat, so each hold clears the reading floor.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle only - if supported, let the hero's soft background glow/blob breathe very slightly with the beat; no waveform bars, no aggressive pulsing
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched - a soft tick per stat counter landing, a light keyboard-tick during the ICP typing beat, a satisfying soft "pop" per ICP tag/content card arrival, a clean whoosh on the harder scene transitions. Nothing cartoonish.
- Audio-coupled moments: stat counters ticking to their final value, the typed business-description line, the ICP card's tags arriving one by one, the glossary/case-study/blog flash-cut sequence
- Restraint rule: no laser/whoosh cliché sound library filler, no music that drowns the SFX, no audio-reactive effect large enough to look like a music visualizer - this is a professional agency, not a party

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - Hook - 3s
Cream background (`#F8F6F2`). "We Fill Your Pipeline" slams in full-black, full-weight, centered, in one hard motion (not a soft fade). ~0.6s after it lands, the gold hand-drawn underline whips in beneath it left-to-right. Hold on the settled line + underline for the remainder of the scene so it's fully readable.
Sequential/interaction: yes - headline lands first (hard), underline draws in second (whip), treated as two beats not one
Audio intent: confident opening hit, no hesitation - this is a claim being made
Audio-coupled idea: underline whip-in lands on a music down-beat; no typing sound here (the whole word arrives at once, this isn't the typing beat)
Music: enters at near-full energy, upbeat pulse established immediately
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 - Proof - 4s
Same cream background. The three stat cards from the real hero stats row arrive left-to-right in quick succession, each with its border/pill treatment: "$120M+ Pipeline Created for Clients", "18 Days Avg. to First Booked Meeting", "12K+ Meetings Booked Annually". Each number visibly counts up to its final value rather than appearing static.
Sequential/interaction: yes - 3 stat cards arrive one by one (not simultaneously), each number counting up on arrival
Audio intent: rapid-fire credibility - each stat should feel like a small hit landing
Audio-coupled idea: soft tick/chime as each counter completes its count-up; ticks land on beat-grid, spaced every-other-beat so each card gets its own moment
Music: steady upbeat pulse continues
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 - Product in motion (ICP Builder) - 6s
Cut to a recreated ICP Builder tool card on the cream/white tool background. A one-line business description types out character-by-character into a real-looking input field (e.g. "B2B SaaS for restaurant inventory"). Cursor/button click on "Generate." An ICP result card assembles: role tag, company-size tag, and pain-point tag snap in one after another, followed by a one-line value-proposition sentence typing out beneath. This is the longest scene - the centerpiece.
Sequential/interaction: yes - text types into the input; on "generate," 3 ICP tags snap in one by one; value-prop line types out last
Audio intent: this is the "wait, it actually works" beat - attentive, slightly anticipatory during typing, satisfying on each tag arrival
Audio-coupled idea: light keyboard-tick during the typed input; soft "pop" per ICP tag snapping in (spaced to clear the reading floor, not beat-crammed); a slightly different, warmer tick for the value-prop line completing
Music: pulls back slightly (small volume duck) so the typing/pop SFX reads clearly, without going fully silent
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Depth flash-cut - 3.5s
Fast sequential flash of the content system: a glossary term card (e.g. "ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)"), a case study card, a blog post card - each snapping into frame and out in quick succession, conveying breadth rather than reading each in full.
Sequential/interaction: yes - 3 cards flash in rapid succession, each held just past the reading floor for its short label (card title only, not full body copy)
Audio intent: quick, energetic momentum build heading into the outro
Audio-coupled idea: a quick soft pop/tick per card, tighter spacing than Scene 3 (this is a flash-cut, not a considered reveal) but never faster than the label can be read
Music: energy ticks back up slightly after the Scene 3 duck
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 - Punchline / outro - 2.5s
Cream background settles. Wordmark "Myntmore" appears center, gold underline echo beneath it (smaller, quieter version of the Scene 1 underline). Below it: "You Close the Deals." Then the real CTA line, "Book Your Free Strategy Call," in the site's actual CTA pill styling. Hold to the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes - wordmark first, tagline second, CTA pill last, each a clean beat
Audio intent: confident landing, not a fade-to-nothing - this should feel like a decision was made
Audio-coupled idea: none required beyond the final musical resolution; a single soft final chime on the CTA pill's arrival is optional
Music: final swell then clean fade on the last held frame
Transition mood: soft crossfade → end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, confident, modern-professional instrumental with light percussive drive - energetic but restrained, never chaotic
**Audio summary:** Music enters at near-full energy under the hook and holds a steady upbeat pulse through the stat reveal, dips slightly under the ICP Builder scene so typing/pop SFX read clearly, ticks back up for the flash-cut, then resolves with a small swell and a clean fade on the final held outro frame.
