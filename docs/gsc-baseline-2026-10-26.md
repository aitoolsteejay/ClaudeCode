## How to read the comparison

- Give changes 3–4 weeks before judging them; Google re-crawls and re-tests titles gradually.
- Judge title changes on click-through at a similar position, and content changes on position.
- Average position can fall while traffic rises, because the site starts showing for broader searches. Look at clicks and at the specific searches above, not the site-wide average.
